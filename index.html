Extreme-GS: Illumination-Consistent 3D Scene Representation under Extreme Lighting

Anonymous Author(s)*
*Anonymous Institution
AAAI 2027
Paper arXiv Code

Extreme-GS recovers illumination-consistent 3D scene representations under extreme lighting conditions.

Abstract

Current novel view synthesis methods rely on multi-view photometric consistency, an assumption frequently violated under extreme lighting where images suffer from over/under-exposure, saturation, and shadows.

We propose Extreme-GS, a self-supervised structured 3D Gaussian framework that introduces a structured shading representation to separate stable scene content from lighting-dependent modulation, with dual-view relighting as self-supervision and illumination-reliability guidance to stabilize geometry optimization.

Method Overview

Extreme-GS takes multi-view images under extreme lighting and decomposes scene appearance into stable content and lighting-dependent modulation via a structured shading representation. Cross-view feature extraction enables dual-view relighting for self-supervision, while illumination-reliability guided optimization (IRGO) suppresses unreliable geometry updates in corrupted regions.
